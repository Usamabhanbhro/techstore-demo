    Design

    Redesign the
    design process.

    3DLive*

    • Speed through reviews.

      Review prototypes faster with teams located around the world — without making physical samples. And with apps like Spatial Analogue, keep stakeholders engaged as they collaborate and iterate on virtual objects.

    • See details in sharp focus.

      View any aspect of internal components with layered 3D models that teams across the globe can use. Platforms like Innoactive help companies like Volkswagen streamline design as they visualize and assess vehicle parts with digital models.

    • Retail refined, with spatial design.

      Give retail designers a new point of view with spatial layouts. Using the Spatial Delivery app, merchandisers can see and modify spaces, rearrange products at scale, and integrate insights on product performance.

    • Get on the same page, product, or prototype.

      Product designers can easily collaborate and review CAD models together with onsite and remote teams. Using PTC’s Onshape Vision app, stakeholders get real-time access to models and components in an immersive workspace.

    Immersive Training

    Simulated training.
    Real results.

    BILT 3D Immersive Instructions*

    • Train teams smarter, wherever they are.

      Provide immersive, true-to-life training on key equipment anytime, anywhere. Industry leaders like CAE use Apple Vision Pro to train pilots in detailed simulations, leading to better retention for full-scale simulator sessions.1

    • Bring a new perspective to learning.

      Easily instruct and simplify learning with lifelike 3D experiences. Apps like Complete HeartX help medical students explore anatomy in stunning detail using photorealistic models, videos, and simulations.

    • Ramp up in record time.

      Accelerate readiness with training modules that reduce errors and speed up tasks. At KLM, technicians use the Engine Shop app to follow step-by-step guidelines overlaid on true-to-scale models of large equipment.

    • Master machines with minimal cost.

      Quickly scale knowledge as employees repair, operate, and maintain physical equipment with spatial overlays. ThingLink makes information visible in an instant, with tutorials and component details overlaid directly onto machinery.

    Guided Work

    Expert guidance
    for complex work.

    Visage Ease VP

    • Keep hands on equipment with hands-free guidance.

      Boost accuracy and speed. Hands-free instruction helps employees keep both hands on their work while using digital reference materials. Technicians at KLM use the Engine Shop app to identify torque sequences, ensuring smooth installations.

    • Streamline surgical training and planning.

      Visualize and train for complex procedures in 3D settings that support skill development and accuracy. Stryker* empowers surgeons beyond the operating room, helping them review patients’ surgical plans in a brilliant, immersive experience.

    • Guide with precision using digital twins.

      Put detailed models next to the real thing, and use a digital step-by-step guide to be more efficient. With the BILT 3D Immersive Instructions app, professionals from builders to electricians complete tasks faster, more accurately, and more safely.

    Customer Experiences

    Turn customer
    awe into action.

    Lowe’s Style Studio

    • Demos shoppers can see — and feel.

      Deliver immersive product demos that help shoppers configure, try, and buy products like never before. At Porsche, customers personalize their dream cars in life size with Apple Vision Pro and experience them in a vivid, delightful way.

    • Elevating customer exploration.

      Build engaging experiences like augmented interior designs, virtual product try-ons, and visuals placed in real-life environments. And with the JigSpace app, manufacturing leaders like EVAPCO can transform customer education.

    • Showcase products without the setup.

      Customers can see, explore, and step inside products virtually. Decathlon’s custom app lets shoppers interact with true-to-scale outdoor equipment without opening a box.

    Productivity

    Infinite workspace.
    Ultimate workflow.

    Mac Virtual Display

    • Powerful, portable, productive.

      Apple Vision Pro creates a workspace that’s like a powerful laptop and ultrawide monitor that can go anywhere — from the office to transit. And when using Mac Virtual Display, the space becomes an infinite canvas for macOS and visionOS apps.

    • One workspace, all the projects.

      Be more productive with custom dashboards for specific tasks, and bring everything into one expansive view. The FieldOps app* by About Objects helps first responders visualize live data and resource allocation to improve situational awareness.

    • Data visualization is state of the chart.

      Bring data to life with depth, clarity, and context. The SAP Analytics Cloud app allows executives and employees to interact with data, seeing it beyond the spreadsheet in an immersive setup.

    A new dimension for business apps.

    Work with everyday business apps in an unlimited, interactive space. Streamline collaboration with Microsoft 365 Copilot, Zoom, and Webex. Accelerate data analysis and visualization at scale with platforms like SAP, Salesforce, and NVIDIA Omniverse. And bring projects and training programs to life with incredible visionOS apps that are ready for enterprise.

    Deploy and manage with confidence.

    It’s simple to add Apple Vision Pro to your existing infrastructure. Easily deploy, secure, and manage it at scale, just like your other Apple devices.

    • Zero-Touch Deployment

      Automatically enroll Apple Vision Pro in device management during initial setup, without IT having to physically touch it.

    • Device Management

      Apple’s built-in frameworks enable IT to configure and monitor devices and deploy apps. With Return to Service, devices can be configured to automatically return to a known state, making shared-use environments easy to manage.

    • Security

      Apple Vision Pro is secure by design, with Apple silicon and the Secure Enclave to protect the system. And Optic ID creates biometric authentication using the unique patterns of an employee’s iris.

    • Identity

      Built-in frameworks for identity integration make signing in to corporate services simple and secure. Users access corporate data with Optic ID, and IT can use management controls to ensure that data stays within managed destinations.

    • Integration

      Apple Vision Pro fits easily into your existing management infrastructure for iPhone and iPad. Access secure Wi-Fi and VPN networks and integrate with file providers in the Files app.
