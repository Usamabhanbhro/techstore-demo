    Environment

    Our planet deserves
    our best thinking.

    Innovation is up.
    Emissions are down.

    We’re closer than ever to Apple 2030: Our ambitious, science-based goal to become carbon neutral across our global footprint. Our aim is to reduce our greenhouse gas emissions by 75% compared with 2015, before balancing the remaining emissions — starting with nature-based solutions that adhere to rigorous international standards.

    Apple’s Restore Fund, California

    A comprehensive approach.
    From design to disassembly.

    • Design and source
    • Make
    • Package and ship
    • Use
    • Recover
    • Carbon removal

    • Design and source. More components in our products are now made with recycled materials, like 100% recycled cobalt in batteries.1

    • Make. In 2025, our Supplier Clean Energy Program generated more than 38 million megawatt-hours.

    • Package and ship. The Studio Display XDR box is designed to collapse into smaller pieces, so it can be easily recycled at home.

    • Use. With just a few simple tools, it’s faster and easier than ever to remove and replace the iPhone battery.

    • Recover. Our robot Daisy can disassemble 36 iPhone models into discrete parts, so we can recover and recycle more materials.

    • Carbon removal. We’ve invested in two dozen conservation and regenerative agriculture projects across six continents.

    and
    made to last.

    We design our products with the environment in mind. That starts by using recycled and responsibly sourced materials.

    MacBook Neo.
    Our lowest-carbon MacBook ever.

    • Material innovation.

      We’ve pioneered methods to transform post-industrial scrap into high-performance aluminum alloys.

      All in on aluminum.

    • Water conservation.

      Through our Supplier Clean Water Program, participating suppliers are reusing water in their factories at an average rate of 43%.

      17B gallons of freshwater saved in 2025.

    • 3D efficiency.

      Apple Watch Ultra 3 and titanium Apple Watch Series 11 cases are 3D printed with 100% recycled aerospace-grade titanium powder — saving more than 400 metric tons of raw titanium.3

      Now made with
      50% less material.

    Packaging and shipping
    that delivers.

    Packaging and shipping
    that delivers.

    To reduce emissions, we’re designing more compact packaging and using lower-carbon modes of transportation where possible.

    • Packaging efficiency.

      iPhone 17 comes in compact packaging, allowing us to ship 35% more products per trip compared to the previous model.4

      Good things come in small packages.

    • Low-carbon transport.

      We prioritize shipping products by ocean, which emits 95% less carbon on average than shipping by air.5

      A sea change in shipping.

    Use. Recover. Reuse.

    Use. Recover. Reuse.

    Making products that last and can be recycled at their end of life helps protect the earth’s precious resources.

    • Longevity and durability.

      We test products against real-world scenarios, like dropping them onto hard surfaces, at our Reliability Testing Labs.

      Products that
      pass the test.

    • Energy efficiency.

      Designed for Apple silicon, Mac mini has a power supply and thermal architecture that improves energy efficiency without compromising performance.

      Chipping away at emissions.

    • Material recovery.

      To recycle more materials, our Advanced Recovery Centers use projector-based AR video systems to guide technicians through device disassembly in real time.

      Disassembly done differently.

    • Clean energy.

      By 2030, our renewable energy projects in Australia will generate over 1 million megawatt-hours of clean electricity annually.6

      Renewable energy. Remarkable scale.
