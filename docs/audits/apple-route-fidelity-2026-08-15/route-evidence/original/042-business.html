    Apple at Work

    Whether you’re a team of one or a global enterprise, Apple gives businesses the tools to do remarkable things. Devices optimized for AI, powerful apps, and integrated services work together, helping you bring your ideas to life.

    Business is better with Mac. Facts.

    • Performance

      MacBook Air is the world’s #1 business laptop.1

      Performance

      MacBook Air is the leading laptop for work thanks to the power of Apple silicon. It delivers incredible performance and up to 18 hours of battery life.2

    • On-Device AI

      57% of enterprise AI models can run locally on MacBook Air.3

      On-Device AI

      With capabilities for inference-based AI and running enterprise models locally, MacBook Air helps keep AI workflows to a fixed cost.

    • Industry Choice

      84% of top U.S. companies use Mac at scale.4

      Industry Choice

      Mac is chosen widely because of its performance, reliability, and chips built for AI. 84% of LinkedIn’s 2026 Top Companies to Grow Your Career in the U.S. use Mac to power their teams.4

    • IT Savings

      Mac generates 55% fewer IT help tickets.5

      IT Savings

      Mac is easy to maintain, with fewer security incidents and 55% fewer IT tickets filed each year. That means lower demand on IT and more room in the budget.5

    • Employee Choice

      MacBook is the #1 laptop among Higher Ed students globally.6

      Employee Choice

      Your future workforce already knows and loves Mac. It’s an incredible tool to boost productivity7 and can even impact employee satisfaction.

    • Yes. Mac, iPhone, and iPad connect to all standard corporate networks. Business apps and services are fully supported, and management controls give IT the flexibility to manage them all with ease.

    • Yes. Apple devices work seamlessly with Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace, so teams can continue using familiar apps like Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook, Teams, Google Docs, and Gmail with fast, secure, cloud-based collaboration. And they have access to thousands more apps on the App Store.

    • With built-in hardware-based security protections, encryption by default, and advanced privacy controls, Apple helps businesses protect data without compromising usability.

      Learn more about Apple Platform Security

    • Apple devices have built-in device management (MDM) frameworks to help businesses deploy apps, customize configurations like Wi-Fi and VPN, and onboard devices effortlessly across their entire fleet. This helps secure devices and safeguard company data if devices are lost or stolen — whether employees work in the office, remotely, or in the field.

      Learn more about Apple Platform Deployment

    • Apple provides migration tools, onboarding resources, and compatibility support to help teams move from PC to Mac smoothly, with minimal disruption to existing workflows.

      Learn more about switching to Mac

    • Apple offers flexible financing options for eligible businesses, making Apple devices more affordable from day one. With payment options aligned to device life cycles, businesses can manage costs, plan refreshes, and maximize budgets over time. Apple devices are built to last and hold their value, with upgrade flexibility built in, helping to lower the total cost of ownership.

    • Apple Business brings together everything organizations need to grow and run efficiently — all in one place. From device management to IT support, team collaboration, and brand promotion, Apple helps businesses stay productive, secure, and connected as they scale.

    • Businesses can choose from a range of support options, including AppleCare, AppleCare for Enterprise, expert technical support at the Apple Store Genius Bar, and access to authorized Apple Technical Partners.
