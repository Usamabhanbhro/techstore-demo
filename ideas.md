# Oroton Homepage Clone — Ground-Truth Reference

This is a reference recreation task, so the supplied Oroton homepage is the chosen design specification. Fidelity to the observed reference takes precedence over introducing a new visual direction.

## Reference

- **Source:** https://oroton.com/
- **Destination route:** `/`
- **Site key:** `oroton-com-8a42`
- **Page key:** `home-6f11`
- **Design movement:** Australian modern luxury editorial retail. The experience balances a restrained monochrome commerce shell with warm, image-led campaign storytelling.
- **Core principles:** generous editorial scale; sparse black-and-white chrome; product-first hierarchy; quiet, tactile materiality.
- **Color philosophy:** a near-white page foundation lets product and campaign photography carry tone. Charcoal typography and thin neutral rules signal luxury without gloss, while warm sand, ochre, and deep red appear only through imagery and collection accents.
- **Layout paradigm:** full-bleed editorial modules alternate with narrow, centered text rails and a horizontal new-in product strip. The page is intentionally image-led rather than card-dashboard based.
- **Signature elements:** compact all-caps utility/header labels; a centered Oroton wordmark; full-bleed campaign tiles with bottom-aligned captions; slim black newsletter/footer band.
- **Interaction philosophy:** navigation and product discovery should feel deliberate and frictionless. Hover reveals are subtle, links use understated underline/opacity changes, and mobile navigation becomes a focused drawer rather than a dense desktop menu.
- **Animation:** use restrained 180–260ms opacity/transform transitions for header, drawers, product hover imagery, and CTA states. Avoid bounce, gradients, or decorative motion that competes with fashion imagery. Respect reduced motion.
- **Typography system:** a high-contrast editorial serif for oversized campaign headlines paired with a neutral sans-serif for navigation, labels, product names, and body copy. Use tracked uppercase labels at small sizes and generous line-height for descriptions.
- **Brand essence:** Australian modern luxury for people who want considered bags, clothing, and accessories with an archival point of view; personality is **refined, tactile, assured**.
- **Brand voice:** concise, confident, and descriptive. Headlines name the edit or collection; CTAs are direct and calm. Example lines: “Uncharted Beauty” and “Discover the new season.”
- **Wordmark & logo:** use a typographic centered “OROTON” lockup with wide tracking and a small geometric woven-mark favicon approximation; do not use a default app logo.
- **Signature brand color:** charcoal black `#161616`, used as the ownable anchor across navigation, type, and footer.

## Constraints

The source page’s live commerce backend, login state, checkout, account data, and inventory are outside this frontend-only recreation. Product interactions are represented as navigable or visually responsive UI without connecting to a real cart.
