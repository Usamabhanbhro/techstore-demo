    Image Quality

    Most def.

    Introducing the all-new Studio Display XDR. With incredible brightness, contrast, and color, this 27-inch 5K Retina XDR display pushes extreme dynamic range further for stunning, true-to-life imagery. It’s a pro-level reference display that’s built to meet the demanding needs of those in film, video, design, and gaming. Welcome to the see suite.

    View the Studio Display XDR Technology White Paper (PDF), opens in new tab

    • Mini-LED backlight.

      2304 dimming zones deliver remarkably precise lighting. For deeper blacks, brighter whites, and reduced halo and blooming.

    • Exceptional brightness and contrast.

      1000 nits SDR is twice as bright as what’s possible with Pro Display XDR, allowing you to work in more varied lighting conditions. 2000 nits peak HDR is 25 percent brighter, for outstanding contrast between highlights and shadows.

    • 120Hz refresh rate and Adaptive Sync.

      Studio Display XDR delivers ultrasmooth motion and can adapt to varying frame rates for greater responsiveness and lower latency. Perfect for gaming.

    • P3 + Adobe RGB.

      This remarkably wide color gamut allows access to both P3 and Adobe RGB color spaces. Essential for designers and photographers who demand accuracy, especially when going to print.

    • Nano-texture. Less reflectivity. More clarity.

      For those who work in particularly bright spaces — inside or out — the nano-texture option reduces reflections to eliminate distractions while maintaining an excellent viewing experience.

    Pro Workflows

    Set your
    screen to stun.

    Studio Display XDR offers calibration and a wide range of reference modes ideal for filmmakers, photographers, graphic designers, and even medical professionals.2 And VFX artists, animators, and game developers will love how the display enhances the detail and responsiveness of their work.

    Adobe Premiere

    Real to reel.

    A variety of reference modes cover the types of projects filmmakers work on, including HDR, HDTV, NTSC, PAL and SECAM, and Digital Cinema.

    Adobe InDesign

    True hues.

    The P3 + Adobe RGB color space delivers incredible color spectrum coverage from screen to print — giving people working in design, publishing, and print access to both P3 and Adobe RGB from the same preset.

    Capture One

    Image conscious.

    Reference mode presets for HDR, P3-D65, and Adobe RGB give photographers choices for the optimal way to view their work.

    Visage 7

    The picture of health.

    With the new medical imaging reference modes and DICOM calibration app, Studio Display XDR can be used to review medical images with outstanding grayscale and color accuracy. And it can easily be switched back to a default preset for general use.2

    Autodesk Flame

    Highly effective.

    Next-level XDR technology helps VFX artists visualize colors, materials, textures, and lighting with incredible fidelity.

    Houdini + Maxon Redshift

    Motion pictures.

    Smooth, accurate motion previews let animators evaluate fast-paced sequences and transitions with frame-by-frame precision.

    Unity Editor

    Game-time precision.

    Adaptive Sync allows refresh rates to vary from 47Hz to 120Hz, lowering input lag and making 3D rendering more responsive.

    Camera

    Everything revolves around you.

    Studio Display XDR features a 12MP Center Stage camera with impressive image quality and low-light performance. Stay perfectly in frame during video calls, even as you move around or others join you. Desk View shows a top-down view of your workspace while keeping you onscreen. And Edge Light illuminates your face by creating a virtual ring light around the edges of the display.

    So they hear you.

    And only you.

    The studio-quality three-mic array makes you the center of attention on video calls and voice recordings. And with directional beamforming, everyone hears you — not what’s going on around you.

    Audio

    Six speakers.
    Soundsational.

    Stream Hijack on the Apple TV app with a subscription

    A six-speaker sound system

    features four force-cancelling woofers for bold bass. And two high-performance tweeters deliver accurate mids and crisp highs. Altogether, this is the highest-fidelity speaker system ever created for Mac.

    Spatial Audio immerses you in sound.

    It’s like having your own personal movie theater or concert hall. When you’re playing music or video with Dolby Atmos, all six speakers work together to create a sophisticated, cinematic soundstage.

    Connectivity

    Plug life.

    Studio Display XDR features two powerful Thunderbolt 5 ports for connecting your Mac, high-speed accessories, or additional displays. Two USB-C ports can be used to connect your external drives, keyboard, or mouse.

    • Daisy-chain a second
      Studio Display XDR

    Houdini, Houdini + Maxon Redshift, Adobe After Effects

    Two Thunderbolt 5 ports. Use one Thunderbolt 5 port to connect your Mac. If you’re using a Mac laptop, a single cable can connect and charge your device simultaneously. Use the other port to daisy-chain an additional Studio Display XDR for a total of over 29 million pixels. Or expand your studio setup by daisy-chaining up to two Studio Display monitors.

    Stand

    Adjust right.

    A tilt- and height-adjustable stand is included with Studio Display XDR, allowing you to effortlessly position your screen at the perfect angle and height. A counterbalancing arm allows for 30 degrees of tilt as well as 105 mm of height adjustment — and makes the display feel weightless as you adjust it.

    VESA mount adapter option. For custom setups, the VESA mount adapter supports landscape and portrait orientation. And it’s compatible with any 100 by 100 mm VESA wall mount, desk mount, stand, or articulating arm.

    the earth in mind.

The standard glass display contains 80 percent recycled glass — an Apple first. And the 100 percent fiber-based box3 has been extensively redesigned for collapsibility, allowing it to be broken down into smaller pieces that will fit in most household recycling bins.
