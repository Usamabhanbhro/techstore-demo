# Product Rail Specification

## Overview

- Target file: `client/src/components/sites/oroton-com-8a42/home-6f11/OrotonProductRail.tsx`
- Interaction model: horizontal discovery rail, hover-driven image and CTA states.

## DOM structure

Section heading row, then a four-up product list. Each product item contains an image button, a small color marker, product name, descriptor, and price.

## Exact style direction

Near-white surface with 44px section padding desktop. Product media uses a soft warm background and 4:5 crop. Copy is sans-serif, 11–13px, with prices slightly stronger in weight.

## Text content

Products include `Mica Mini Bowler`, `Olivia Day Bag`, `Etta Shoulder Bag`, and `Spot Scarf Print`. Supporting labels use real source phrases such as `New In` and `Crafted for the everyday`.

## Responsive behavior

Desktop presents four columns. Tablet presents two columns. Mobile becomes a two-column horizontal rail with overflow scrolling and hidden scrollbars.
