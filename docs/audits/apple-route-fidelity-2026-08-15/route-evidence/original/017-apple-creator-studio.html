    Final Cut Pro

    The ultimate
    video
    creation experience.

    Craft with power and precision using Final Cut Pro on Mac and iPad. With intuitive tools designed to elevate every frame, your vision moves from concept to cinematic story at the speed of thought.

    Learn more about Final Cut Pro

    • Start-to-finish performance.

      Shoot pro-level footage with complete control in Final Cut Camera on iPhone.5 Then edit on iPad or Mac with powerful and approachable tools and effects, fine-tune the audio, and share your finished story — all in one seamless flow.

    • Instantly find the moment you’re looking for.

      Track down a shot without scrubbing through footage using Visual Search6 and locate the perfect sound bite with only a few keywords using Transcript Search.7

    • Video editing with speed and flexibility.

      The Magnetic Timeline lets you move and trim freely while maintaining synchronization so you can explore endless creative possibilities. Connected Clips and storylines hold your edit together. Multicam helps you cut the perfect interview. And Auditions let you find the best take.

    • Perfect timing, every time.

      Beat Detection matches your music’s rhythm so you can align clips effortlessly and keep your edits in sync with the beat.

    • Beautiful, customizable graphics.

      Add professionally designed motion graphics to your project with Motion in Apple Creator Studio, or select graphics from third-party providers like MotionVFX, FxFactory, and more.8

    • Fast delivery.

      Quickly batch export files that are optimized for any size — from your iPhone screen to the big screen. You can even use Compressor to customize export settings using a variety of pro formats.

    Pixelmator Pro

    Creative imaging and design.
    Made for everyone.

    Edit and design using intelligent tools that bring your vision to life. From social posts to billboards, Pixelmator Pro simplifies every step of your projects. Effortlessly customize vector designs or typography, then let powerful features help your imagination take shape.

    Learn more about Pixelmator Pro

    • Speed up your workflow.

      Intelligent features automate image edits like background removal, image repair, detail enhancement, color adjustment, and more.

    • Layering without limits.

      Use simple building blocks — shape, text, video, and image layers — to create virtually anything you can imagine. Add effects, adjust composition, and experiment freely with nondestructive editing, knowing every change is reversible.

    • Stunning templates and mock-ups.

      Start working from professionally crafted templates and beautiful mock-ups for social media and print.

    • Typographic control.

      Use any font and customize your text with everything from sophisticated styles to curved paths. Preview it live and save your favorite styles as presets.

    • Fully optimized for touch on iPad.

      Use natural gestures for fast, fluid onscreen interactions. And with Apple Pencil, you can edit with even more precision, so your creative work is smooth and intuitive.9

    Logic Pro

    The fast track to the final mix.
    Wherever you go.

    Create with intelligent Session Players, cutting-edge beat-making tools, and a massive Sound Library — seamlessly across Mac and iPad.10 Logic Pro makes music production easier than ever.

    Learn more about Logic Pro

    • Music production with AI Session Players.11

      Produce with a full band of musicians that can lay down polished synth, bass, keyboard, and drums that adapt in real time to your musical style.

    • From idea to final master, fast.

      Supercharge your workflow with intelligent tools, like Stem Splitter and Mastering Assistant, to help you fine-tune your creative process so you can produce, refine, and finish in no time.

    • Record drafts on the go.

      Voice Memos lets you record anywhere using your iPhone, iPad, Mac, or Apple Watch. Then bring your recording straight into Logic Pro to take your music to the next level.

    • Beat-making tools.

      Build custom drum kits with any samples or instruments, then easily reshape melodies and rhythms with Step Sequencer and craft the perfect beat.

    • Massive Sound Library.

      From loops to samples to patches, get thousands of royalty-free sounds in an ever-growing collection, so you can build tracks without boundaries.

    • Instruments and effects.

      Dial up authentic vintage keyboards or powerful modern synths to give your tracks a distinctive vibe. And explore a complete suite of effect plug-ins for detailed sound shaping and mixing.

    Questions? Answers.

    • You can download all the apps included in Apple Creator Studio from the App Store. It comes with Final Cut Pro, Logic Pro, and Pixelmator Pro as well as Motion, Compressor, MainStage, and other tools. You also get premium content with powerful intelligence in Keynote, Pages, Numbers, and Freeform.

    • Apple Creator Studio gives subscribers access to a suite of creative apps designed for video, music, and image editing and creation. You can make videos in Final Cut Pro, Motion, and Compressor, produce music in Logic Pro and MainStage, and edit photos or graphics in Pixelmator Pro. And while Keynote, Pages, Numbers, and Freeform remain free for everyone, an Apple Creator Studio subscription offers premium templates, a library of high-quality, royalty-free photos and graphics, and powerful intelligence features.

    • Yes. If you already own Final Cut Pro, Logic Pro, Pixelmator Pro, MainStage, Motion, or Compressor, you can continue using them. These standalone Mac apps are also available for one-time purchase and redownload from the App Store, so you can keep working with the tools you have.

    • Yes. You can continue using Keynote, Pages, Numbers, and Freeform for free. And while these apps remain free for everyone, an Apple Creator Studio subscription offers premium templates, a library of high-quality, royalty-free photos and graphics, and powerful intelligence features.

    • All the projects and content you create with an active subscription to Apple Creator Studio — including any images you generate or add from the Content Hub — remain licensed in the context of your original creation.

      Projects in Final Cut Pro, Logic Pro, and Pixelmator Pro remain on all your devices, and you can copy or share them to any other device. To open or edit a project, you need to be an active subscriber. Keynote, Pages, Numbers, and Freeform documents remain unchanged and can be edited; however, no new edits using paid features will be possible.

    • For the optimal experience, full functionality is available with macOS 26, iPadOS 26, and iOS 26 and later. Apple Creator Studio requires an Apple Account and internet connection to subscribe.

      Logic Pro requires macOS 15.6 or later, iPadOS 26 or later, a Mac with Apple silicon, and an iPad with Apple A12 Bionic chip or later.

      Pixelmator Pro requires a Mac with macOS 26 or later or an iPad with Apple M1 chip or later, iPad (A16), or iPad mini (A17 Pro) and iPadOS 26 or later.

      Keynote, Pages, and Numbers require macOS 15.6 or later, iPadOS 18 or later, iOS 18 or later, or visionOS 2 or later. Some premium features require macOS 26, iPadOS 26, iOS 26, or visionOS 26 or later.

      Motion requires macOS 15.6 or later.

      Compressor requires macOS 15.6 or later. Some features require a Mac with Apple silicon.

      MainStage requires macOS 15.6 or later.

    • Yes, a standard Apple Creator Studio subscription can be shared with up to five other family members using Family Sharing.

      Educator and student subscriptions are for individual use and cannot be shared through Family Sharing.
