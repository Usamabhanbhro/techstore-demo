## Accessories taxonomy from the official Accessories page

The official Accessories page lists Shop by Product: iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch, Apple Vision Pro, AirPods, TV & Home, AirTag, and Beats. It also lists Shop by Category: Gaming, Mice & Keyboards, Smart Home Accessories, Office Accessories, Cases & Protection, Content Creation, Headphones & Speakers, Chargers & Adapters, Software, Hard Drives & Storage, and Health & Fitness. The implementation preserves these meaningful categories and adds only source-supported subcategories such as Apple Pencil, MagSafe, Watch Bands, and audio accessories.
