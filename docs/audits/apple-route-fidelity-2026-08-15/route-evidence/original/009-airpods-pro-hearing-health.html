    Hearing Health

    Pioneering hearing.

    Access a Hearing Test,1 a Hearing Aid feature,1 and active Hearing Protection2 using just AirPods Pro 3 or AirPods Pro 2 and an iPhone or iPad. It’s the world’s first end‑to‑end hearing health experience.

    The Hearing Test and Hearing Aid features are regulated health features that require approval and will be offered after authorization is received. Feature availability varies by region.

    View complete list

    Awareness

    Take a Hearing Test. Right at home.

    Millions of people are unaware that they’re living with hearing loss, and even more don’t get tested. But now it’s faster, easier, and more convenient than ever to get scientifically validated results on your own time.1

    How to take a Hearing Test

    A more convenient process. Active Noise Cancellation and the in-ear seal on AirPods Pro simulate the conditions needed to evaluate and provide you with insights into your hearing. Just find a quiet, comfortable place and launch the test from your AirPods settings or the Health app. The system will automatically check the fit of your AirPods Pro and assess background noise levels before getting started.

    Hear the tones, tap the screen. Using pure‑tone audiometry built on advanced acoustic science, the test plays a series of tones that you respond to by tapping the screen. Helpful animations, tap‑back effects, and progress indicators guide you through the experience, which takes about five minutes. More than 150,000 real‑world audiograms and millions of simulations helped develop a Hearing Test that’s accurate, fast, and easy.

    Immediate results. Seamless support. When you complete the test, you see a personalized profile with easy‑to‑understand results and insights about your hearing. If you have little to no loss, but could still benefit from hearing certain frequencies more clearly, you’ll be able to adjust your Media Assist settings for enhanced audio on media and calls. And if you have perceived mild to moderate hearing loss, you’ll be prompted to set up the Hearing Aid feature.

    Private, secure, and easy to manage. All your Hearing Test data is privately and securely stored in the Health app on your device. You can retake the test whenever you want, monitor your hearing over time, download your test results, and read helpful articles about hearing health.

    Assistance

    A clinical‑grade Hearing Aid.

    AirPods Pro 3 and AirPods Pro 2 provide the world’s first software-enabled Hearing Aid feature1 that is remarkably easy to use throughout the day. Now with 67 percent more battery life in Transparency on a single charge with AirPods Pro 3.3 Simply switch it on and hear it all clearly.

    Learn about setting up the Hearing Aid feature

    A meaningful difference. After you complete the Hearing Test, if you have mild to moderate hearing loss, your personalized hearing profile will guide you to the Hearing Aid feature. With a quick setup, you’ll start hearing more clearly immediately. You can even customize the settings to your needs.

    Up to 10 hours of battery life using the Hearing Aid feature.3

    Powered by Apple-designed audio innovations. With advanced computational audio and the powerful H2 chip, the Hearing Aid feature boosts frequencies so sounds are clearer and more vibrant to your ears. Improved voice clarity and automatic Conversation Boost with AirPods Pro 3 make communicating in louder environments easier than ever.4 And thanks to computational audio, you’ll get real-time adjustments as you go about your day.

    Prevention

    Hearing Protection for daily life.

    AirPods Pro 3 and AirPods Pro 2 provide active Hearing Protection2 across listening modes to help prevent exposure to loud environmental noise.

    1 in 3 people experience exposure to loud noise on a regular basis.

    Mowing the lawn 90 dBA

    City bus or subway 95 dBA

    Fitness classes 100 dBA

    Live events 105 dBA

    Take noise down a notch. Hearing Protection translates the sound signatures of what you hear to lower levels in environments as loud as 110 dBA. Machine learning on the H2 chip actively reduces louder, more intermittent noise at 48,000 times per second, and the ear tips provide passive noise reduction.

    More music to your ears. When you’re enjoying a live concert, the improved high dynamic range multiband compressor helps protect your hearing while keeping the music sounding natural and vibrant.5

    More intelligent ways to listen.

    AirPods Pro 3 and AirPods Pro 2 include a range of features that support and enhance your hearing in a variety of environments, settings, and situations.

    The world's best in-ear Active Noise Cancellation with AirPods Pro 3 removes up to 2x more background noise before it reaches your ears.6

    Adaptive Audio intelligently tailors noise control to changes in your environment throughout the day.7

    Adaptive EQ tunes the listening experience to your ears in real time based on the fit of your AirPods Pro 3 or AirPods Pro 2.

    Personalized Volume learns your listening preferences and adjusts the volume in response to your surroundings.

    Background Sounds play ambient sounds — like the ocean or rain — that can help minimize distractions.

    Headphone Audio Levels let you set a volume limit and track how long and how often you listen to loud audio.

    Apple Footer

    1. The Hearing Aid feature has received FDA authorization. The Hearing Test and Hearing Aid features are supported on AirPods Pro 2 and later with the latest firmware when paired with a compatible iPhone or iPad with iOS 18 or iPadOS 18 and later and are intended for people 18 years old or older. The Hearing Aid feature is also supported on a compatible Mac with macOS Sequoia and later. It is intended for people with perceived mild to moderate hearing loss.
    2. The Hearing Protection feature works with AirPods Pro 2 and later with the latest firmware when paired with a compatible iPhone, iPad, or Mac with iOS 18, iPadOS 18, or macOS Sequoia and later. The feature is not available in all regions or all languages. View complete list. See support.apple.com/120850 for total attenuation and more information. The Hearing Protection feature is not suitable for protection against extremely loud impulse sounds, such as gunfire, fireworks, or jackhammers, or against sustained sounds louder than 110 dBA.
    3. 67% more battery life compared with AirPods Pro 2. Testing conducted by Apple in July and August 2025 using preproduction AirPods Pro 3 with MagSafe Charging Case paired with preproduction iPhone 17 Pro units, all with prerelease software. Both volume and amplification level were set to 50% and Transparency was enabled. Testing consisted of full AirPods Pro battery discharge with the Hearing Aid feature on, with no audio playing back from iPhone, until the first AirPod Pro was fully discharged. Battery life depends on device settings, environment, usage, and many other factors.
    4. Supported on AirPods Pro 2 and later with the latest firmware when paired with a compatible iPhone running iOS 26 and later. This feature may not be available in all regions or all languages. View complete list.
    5. In a 105 dBA environment, when used as directed, the Hearing Protection feature in Transparency mode provides an exposure limit of (a) 2.5 to 6 hours / 7 days for most users with AirPods Pro 2, and (b) 6 to 16 hours / 7 days for most users with AirPods Pro 3. Exposure limit is derived from World Health Organization recommendations, and varies with listening mode, fit, and environmental sound level. See support.apple.com/120850 for more information.
    6. 2x more Active Noise Cancellation compared with AirPods Pro 2. World's best Active Noise Cancellation evaluation tested in July 2025 in accordance with IEC 60268-24 as compared with best-selling commercially available wireless in-ear headphones.
    7. Available on compatible devices running iOS 18, iPadOS 18, or macOS Sequoia and later when paired with AirPods 4 with Active Noise Cancellation or AirPods Pro 2 and later with the latest firmware.
