    Designed to Engage

    Top marks for
    empowered learning.

    With Mac and iPad, engagement comes naturally with a seamless, intuitive experience.

    • All the apps educators already love.

      iPad and Mac are compatible with apps that teachers and students use every day, like Google Workspace for Education with Gemini, Microsoft 365 Copilot, and Adobe Photoshop, so lessons stay on track, without missing a beat.

    • Built for engagement, inside and out.

      With iPad, anywhere can be a classroom. Its lightweight design and all-day battery life let students take learning on the go — from documenting plant life cycles to taking notes on a field trip.2

      Learn more about iPad

    • Versatile tools turn learning into doing.

      From book reports to student films to presentations, Mac and iPad become anything learners need them to be. And Keynote, Pages, iMovie, and GarageBand are built right in.

      Learn more about built-in apps

    • Work flows easily across iPad and Mac.

      Apple devices work together to keep class running smoothly. Educators can move seamlessly from projects on iPad to Mac, supporting students in real time.

      Learn more about Continuity

    • Real skills for the real world.

      When learners use Apple devices, they build real-world skills like problem-solving and collaboration. In a study, 81 percent of teachers surveyed reported that Mac had a positive impact on students’ critical thinking skills.1

      Read the impact study, opens in new tab

    • Assessment tools that pass the test.

      Apple devices are supported by most summative and interim exam vendors, giving educators the tools to track student progress and provide support along the way.

      Learn more about assessment tools (PDF), opens in new tab

    • The classroom, all in one view.

      The Classroom app gives teachers a live view of each student’s screen so they can guide students to the right page, keep lessons on track, and share resources.

      Learn more about teaching tools

    Designed for Expression

    Creative tools.
    Personal touch.

    Apple helps students learn and express ideas in the ways that work best for them.

    • Apps for learning — their way.

      Built-in apps like iMovie and GarageBand put creative power in every student’s hands. Whether it’s a news podcast, a short film, or an original soundscape, there’s an app to make it happen.

      Learn more about creativity apps

    • Get ideas flowing with Apple Pencil.

      From sketching science fair ideas in Freeform to creating hand-drawn illustrations in Notes or Pages, Apple Pencil gives students a natural, expressive way to learn and bring ideas to life.

      Learn more about Apple Pencil

    • Explore any angle with AR.

      With augmented reality on iPad, students can visualize and interact with abstract concepts like molecular structures or muscular systems — making complex ideas tangible.

      Learn more about augmented reality

    • Make learning accessible to all.

      Students can create, communicate, and explore on their own terms with built-in accessibility features like VoiceOver, Live Captions, and AssistiveTouch.

      Learn more about accessibility

    Bring app development to any classroom.

    With Swift Playground and Xcode, students can bring their ideas and perspectives to coding and app development — growing their confidence and problem-solving skills. And Apple gives teachers the tools to support students, no matter the experience level.

    Learn more about coding

    Designed to Last

    A smart investment,
    year after year.

    Powerful, durable, and AI-ready. Apple devices keep you ahead of the curve, while staying private and secure.

    Privacy protections built in.

    Apple products and services are designed to help keep student data safe and secure, with limited data collection, on-device AI processing, and control over how personal information is shared.

    Performance at the top of the class.

    Apple silicon delivers incredible performance, speed, and all-day battery life so you can take on ambitious projects or multitask with several apps — even when unplugged.2

    Ready for what AI brings next.

    Apple silicon delivers powerful on-device AI, driving today’s big ideas and the ones that come next — all while protecting personal data.

    Devices that go the distance.

    Apple devices are designed to handle years of everyday use. And with recyclable materials, you’re investing in tools that are good for students and good for the planet.

    Free, automatic software updates.

    Regular macOS and iPadOS updates deliver new features and help ensure that devices run smoothly and securely for years to come.

    Low maintenance, high value.

    With fewer support tickets, lower maintenance costs, and devices that hold their value for trade-in, Mac and iPad reduce the cost of ownership over time.1

    Streamlined deployment.
    Seamless management.

    Whether you’re bringing Apple devices to one department or an entire district, we make it easier for IT teams to set up and manage devices at scale.

    Zero-touch deployment. Zero worries.

    With Apple School Manager, IT teams can enroll devices in device management services without needing to physically touch them. And flexible deployment models support personal devices, Shared iPad, Mac labs, and Apple TV at scale.

    Read the Education Deployment Guide

    Manage devices from anywhere.

    After deploying devices, Apple School Manager and device management solutions enable IT to remotely configure devices, manage dozens of settings, and easily purchase and distribute apps and books.

    Seamless integration with Apple School Manager.

    Easily integrate with Google Workspace, Microsoft Entra ID, or your own identity provider so students can use the login and password they already know. And with a direct connection to your Student Information System, automatically populate the Classroom and Schoolwork apps with current rosters.

    Learn more about Apple School Manager

    200GB of iCloud storage. Free.

    Staff and students with a school-owned Managed Apple Account get access to 200GB of storage for assignments, resources, and documents.
