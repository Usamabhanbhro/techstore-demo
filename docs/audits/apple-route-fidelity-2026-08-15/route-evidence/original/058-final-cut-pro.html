    Video Editing

    Storytelling, simplified.

    Whether you’re new to editing or a seasoned pro, Final Cut Pro makes it easy to move and trim clips, isolate without a green screen, and create captions. With smart, intuitive tools that smooth your workflow, video editing has never been this effortless.

    • Magnetic Timeline for fast, flexible editing.

      Experiment with different edits and story ideas by moving and trimming clips, all while keeping everything in sync.

    • Isolate with Magnetic Mask.

      Separate people and objects from the background without a green screen or complicated rotoscoping.

    • Automatic captions.

      By analyzing spoken audio, Final Cut Pro creates captions with lightning-fast, accurate results.

    Find any shot in a snap.

    Search visual moments. With Visual Search, locating clips is effortless. Just type what you’re looking for and it pinpoints objects and actions in seconds.4

    Grab the perfect sound bite. No more sifting through hours of footage. Transcript Search analyzes speech to instantly find spoken phrases, whether by exact match or by related words.5

    Intelligence

    Scene smarts.

    Edit with AI features that build on Apple Intelligence.2 Final Cut Pro transforms video creation, from streamlining complex tasks to enhancing visuals. It helps you move quickly from first cut to final delivery, all with pro‑quality results.

    • Automatically track movement.

      Object Tracker maps the movement of people, objects, or faces so you can attach effects and titles or make adjustments as they move through the shot.

    • One-click color grading.

      Automatically enhance the color balance, contrast, and brightness of video or still images with the Enhance Light and Color effect, powered by machine learning.

    • Turn video into slow motion.

      Smooth Slo-Mo intelligently generates and blends video frames to provide the highest-quality movement and add more drama to a project.

    • Automatically reframe a clip.

      Your videos adjust seamlessly to fit every aspect ratio, so that they’re ready to share on Mac, iPhone, iPad, and all your favorite social platforms.

    Get in the groove with Beat Detection.

    All-new Beat Detection automatically analyzes music so you can map out song parts, bars, and beats in a grid. Then just drop in your clips and snap them to the rhythm. Your edits hit right on time, and manual syncing is a distant memory.

    • Dynamic titles.

      Customize themed titles, lower thirds, and bumpers to enhance your project.

    Motion

    All the right moves.

    Easily design cinematic 2D, 3D, and 360° titles, smooth transitions, and realistic effects in real time. Optimized for Apple silicon and the Neural Engine, Motion lets you create and play back stunning visuals with incredible speed and precision.

    Motion Graphics

    More wow. Less work.

    Designed with editors in mind, the streamlined interface and remarkable performance of Motion let you create and play back titles, transitions, and effects — all in real time.

    Quickly find and organize assets, then build motion graphics with layers, a full timeline, and a keyframe editor, using a modern and easily customizable interface.

    Create titles, transitions, generators, and filters. Save and publish them to Final Cut Pro, and instantly jump back to Motion at any time for more advanced changes.

    Design Smart Motion Templates that include USDZ 3D models. Publish any parameter or create rigs that can control several at once. Set up templates with multiple aspect ratios, and Final Cut Pro automatically uses the correct layout.

    Access thousands of third-party plug-ins and templates for tracking, 3D object creation, titles, transitions, and effects.

    Thanks to the speed and efficiency of Metal architecture, Motion can play back what you create in real time. The Media Engine in Apple silicon accelerates ProRes playback and export, freeing up power for particle rendering and complex behaviors. And the Neural Engine makes object and face tracking lightning fast.

    Design Tools

    Real‑time animation. A real time‑saver.

    Motion features a real-time design engine that lets you see your work instantly, along with a deep set of tools and content for creating and animating complex motion graphics.

    Build captivating 2D, 3D, and 360° compositions by choosing from more than 1,900 Apple-designed, royalty-free graphics including vector artwork, images, animations, and 60 USDZ 3D models.

    Easily fine-tune timing and movement with intuitive keyframe tools. Use curve interpolation for smooth changes or draw curves using a freehand tool. Or move, stretch, and condense groups of keyframes using the Transform box.

    Add natural motion with preset effects like Gravity, Throw, and Vortex — or combine them for more advanced animations.

    Build custom animated backgrounds with generators you can style, texture, or apply to 3D text.

    2D and 3D Text

    Effortless titles. Spread the word.

    Make beautiful 2D and 3D titles that you can animate with drag-and-drop actions and intuitive text animation tools.

    Create text with your favorite fonts and adjust position, opacity, and rotation. Manipulate vector-based characters with pristine sharpness, and use presets to easily add complex animations.

    Build 3D titles from scratch, start from templates, or instantly convert any 2D title to 3D. Customize your 3D text with over 90 Apple-designed organic and artificial materials. You can even use lighting rigs or depth-of-field effects to make titles perfectly match their environments.

    Choose from more than 100 animation presets, including Type On, Blur Out, and Text-on-a-Path. Animate text on or off the screen in a snap, or create unique animations by moving letters exactly where you want.

    Automate tasks that would otherwise take hours to complete. With generators, you can count up or down, randomize text, and add timecode sequences.

    Quickly set up scrolling credits, even for long lists. Import a text file or type credits directly into your project, then use presets to automatically animate the speed of the credits.

    Effects

    Drag. Drop. Delight.

    With over 200 filters and effects built into Motion, you can quickly and easily assemble impressive animations and fine-tune your work with precise controls.

    Machine learning automatically detects and tracks faces or objects. Attach images, particles, filters, paint strokes, or text to create eye-catching visuals.

    From smoke to sparkles, explore more than 200 particle presets or create your own designs. Arrange them into striking 2D and 3D patterns with replicators, then add 3D objects for extra impact.

    Choose from over 140 brush presets, or craft your own using gradients or moving video textures. Paint with pressure-sensitive brushstrokes and make vector-based strokes that flow naturally in 3D space.

    Image stabilization lets you quickly smooth shaky footage. And SmoothCam removes jitters and bumps while retaining pans, tilts, and zooms.

    Create an accurate chroma key with the drag-and-drop Keying filter. Adjust uneven lighting with advanced tools like the color wheel. And preview results instantly without rendering.

    FxPlug in. Stand out.

    A powerful Apple-designed plug-in architecture for filters and effects, FxPlug features more than 130 built-in options along with a wide range of third-party choices for Motion and Final Cut Pro. The FxPlug ecosystem continues to grow, with FxPlug 4 plug-ins running seamlessly on both Intel- and Apple silicon–based Mac computers.

    Easier audio? That tracks.

    It’s easy to get your audio sounding just right. With sound editing tools for cleaning up dialogue, customizing soundtracks, and adding pro-level effects, Final Cut Pro lets you shape your sound directly in the timeline — or use Logic Pro for even more control. Whether it’s a quick fix or a full mix, your audio is always in good hands.

    • Audio editing tools.

      Choose from a library of plug-ins for audio compression, EQ, and more adventurous effects. Or export to a pro audio app like Logic Pro for advanced audio mixing.

    • Use effects from Logic Pro.

      No need to switch apps. Final Cut Pro includes a range of powerful audio plug-ins from Logic Pro, along with third-party audio plug‑ins.6

    • Voice Isolation.

      Eliminate background noise and improve clarity of speech so your edit sounds pristine.

    • Fit the music to the cut with iPad.

      Soundtracks dynamically adjust to fit the length of your project — expanding, shifting, and staying in sync as a scene evolves.

    Workflows

    Fast Floward.

    Thanks to advanced workflows, your process goes faster with less effort. From high-performance editing to powerful export options, projects move smoothly all the way to delivery. No slowdowns. No bottlenecks. Just flow.

    • Optimize files.

      Transcode video to Apple ProRes for better performance while editing, faster render times, and enhanced color quality for compositing.

    • Share in high quality.

      Export your completed project as a professional-quality file that can be used for the final stages of post-production or broadcast and distribution.

    • Batch export.

      Export multiple projects, clips, or clip ranges simultaneously for different destinations, such as mobile devices or social media.

    Compressor. Expand your encoding options.

    Create custom export settings, apply filters, and take full control of your video output. Compressor expands on high-performance encoding options with support for multiple industry-standard formats like HDR, HEVC, 360° video, and MXF. It’s the most powerful and flexible way to deliver your Final Cut Pro projects, no matter where they’re headed.

    Compressor

    Export like an expert.

    Compressor works seamlessly with Final Cut Pro, letting you add custom output settings for HDR, HEVC, MXF, and 360° and spatial video. Designed for Apple silicon, it’s the most powerful and flexible way to export and deliver your projects.

    Performance

    Efficient to its core.

    With standout performance and optimizations for the latest generation of Mac computers, Compressor is the perfect companion for custom encoding.

    High-performance encoding. A powerful Metal engine takes advantage of the CPU, GPU, and memory in your Mac to accelerate encoding of high-resolution video. Compressor leverages hardware acceleration for H.264 and HEVC encoding, spreads work across CPU cores, and uses the GPU to speed up demanding tasks.

    Optimized for Apple silicon. No matter which Mac you’re using, Compressor delivers lightning-fast encoding up to 8K and significantly faster ProRes, HEVC, and H.264 processing, thanks to the Apple Media Engine.

    Features

    Industry standards come standard.

    With support for a wide range of popular device outputs as well as professional video and audio formats, Compressor expands on the encoding options built into Final Cut Pro.

    HDR. View HDR footage on any Mac that displays an extended range of brightness, and see the video right in the viewer before starting a batch export.

    HEVC. Export in HEVC for high-quality files that are up to 40 percent smaller than H.264 videos. Adjust output parameters, then save your settings and access them directly in Final Cut Pro.

    Closed captions. Easily view, adjust, and export captions in multiple formats. Embed caption metadata or deliver separate sidecar files for flexible workflows.

    Audio descriptions. Embed audio descriptions into MOV, MP4, M4V, or MXF formats to make your videos accessible to more audiences.

    360 VR export. Export 360° video with industry‑standard spherical metadata embedded in the files.

    Encode for all platforms. Use built-in presets to deliver optimized files for iPhone, iPad, and Apple TV, or customize settings to your liking. Export files for social media platforms, including YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, and Vimeo.

    Camera log conversion. Convert log footage to HDR or SDR using manufacturer LUTs. And with batch export, you can process hundreds of dailies with just a few clicks.

    Custom LUTs. Add a stylized look to your footage using custom LUTs. Work in .cube, .mga, and .m3d formats, all available on stock sites or generated from apps like Pixelmator Pro.

    MXF encoding. Create MXF files with codecs including AVC‑Intra, D‑10/IMX, ProRes, and XDCAM HD. Adjust parameters and choose from a variety of frame rates and resolutions.

    Broad format support. Quickly encode in multiple industry-standard formats, including MPEG-2, H.264, HEVC, QuickTime, and MXF. Transcode Sony X-OCN or Canon Cinema RAW Light using a third-party plug-in, add captions, and package HLS-ready files for online streaming.

    iTunes Store Package

    From your screen to seen.

    Compressor makes it simple to package your film for submission to the iTunes Store.

    Add content. Import and organize movies, trailers, captions, and subtitles. Use the inspector to view details and add metadata.

    Audition. Preview video with any audio, caption, or subtitle. Continuous playback lets you switch tracks while checking sync.

    Package. Easily assemble all your assets with the option to use your ProRes source files without additional compression. Reassign audio and add metadata with automatic subtitle detection.

    Accessibility. Add captions and audio descriptions so your videos can reach a wider audience.

    Validate. Built-in error detection flags issues. Select the error or warning to jump directly to its source.

    Submit. Compress your package and send it to an Apple‑certified partner for iTunes Store distribution and sale.

    iPad

    Your studio. To go.

    Final Cut Pro brings powerful video editing to iPad, combining pro features with the freedom of a touch-first experience. Whether you’re capturing content on the go or editing on set, turning inspiration into creation is just a tap away.

    Connect up to four iPhone or iPad devices, control their settings remotely, and automatically sync all your footage. Easily record music videos, podcasts, and interviews.

    Capture professional‑level footage on your iPhone or iPad with powerful manual controls including ISO, shutter speed, white balance, and more.7

    Make frame-accurate edits with just a tap of your finger. Or spin the lightning-fast jog wheel to zip through footage and fine-tune your clips.

    Apply precise edits using Apple Pencil.8 And with Apple Pencil Pro,9 you can squeeze to open the tool palette and use barrel roll to rotate your brush tip in Live Drawing.

    Draw, highlight, and write directly on footage with touch or Apple Pencil to create unique, animated titles and illustrations.

    Montage Maker automatically selects the best visual highlights from your footage and cuts them to the beat of any song. Then you can fine-tune the pacing, make a few tweaks, and share away.

    Export your iPad project to Mac to access features like advanced color grading, Magnetic Mask, and more.
