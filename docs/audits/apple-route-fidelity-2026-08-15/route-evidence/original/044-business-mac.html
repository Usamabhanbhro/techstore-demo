    Apple at Work

    Mac for Business

    Mac
    does  that.

    Whether you’re a small business or an enterprise, it’s an incredible time to put the power of Mac to work — at work. Mac can be deployed in minutes. It runs the apps your teams use every day. And it offers rock-solid security. See what Mac can do for your business.

    Brains meet brawn meet business.

    • A workhorse for

      your workforce.

      Performance

      A workhorse for your workforce. Thanks to Apple silicon, Mac brings game-changing power and speed to everything you do — from big data processing to machine learning tasks. So whether you’re taking on the everyday or your next monumental project, Mac can help you do virtually anything, faster.

    • Built for AI. From daily
      tasks to LLM asks.

      AI

      Built for AI. From daily tasks to LLM asks. Incredible performance, built-in privacy protections, and an intuitive user experience are just a few of the reasons Mac is the best AI PC for work.

      • Integrated. And optimized. With AI‑optimized hardware, powerful software integration, and a robust ecosystem of apps, Mac can crush everyday needs as well as highly demanding ones.

      • Apple silicon. Mac with Apple silicon features a Neural Engine and unified memory for efficient handling of sophisticated AI processing.

      • Privacy and security. Advanced privacy and security are built in to help protect your company‘s data. And on Mac, you can run AI on device, allowing you to keep inputs and results private.

      • A world of apps. An entire catalog of AI-ready apps is available on Mac, iPhone, and iPad, so you can boost your teams’ capabilities right away — and work seamlessly across devices.

    • Up to 24 hours
      of battery life.4

      Battery

      Up to 24 hours of battery life. With all-day battery life, Mac won’t quit until you do. It brings the same outstanding performance whether it’s plugged in at the office or you’re working on the go.4 And with fast charging, your MacBook Air or MacBook Pro can charge up to 50 percent in around 30 minutes.5

    • Apple Intelligence.
      Personal, private, powerful.

      Apple Intelligence

      Apple Intelligence. Personal, private, powerful. Every Mac comes with Apple Intelligence, a personal intelligence system that helps your employees write, express themselves, and get things done effortlessly. With groundbreaking privacy and security protections, it gives you peace of mind that no one else can access your company data — not even Apple.7

      • Write, refine, focus. Apple Intelligence brings systemwide Writing Tools to macOS, helping employees rewrite, proofread, and summarize text with ease. Whether they’re drafting emails in Mail, capturing ideas in Notes, or working in third-party apps, employees can communicate more clearly and efficiently. Intelligent notification prioritization helps them stay focused and minimize distractions — so they can get more done with less effort.

      • The start of a new era for Siri. Siri draws on Apple Intelligence for new superpowers. With an all-new design, richer language understanding, and the ability to type to Siri whenever it’s convenient, employees can interact in whatever way suits the moment. And with extensive product knowledge and the ability to tap into ChatGPT when it might be useful to answer a question, Siri helps your teams get answers, solve problems, and stay productive.

      • Expressive tools for work. Image Playground makes it easy to generate original visuals based on text prompts or photos — perfect for presentations, brainstorming, or storytelling. And with Genmoji, teams can create expressive, personalized emoji to add clarity, tone, or a human touch to conversations. Whether collaborating in Messages or building content in apps like Freeform and Keynote, Apple Intelligence helps bring ideas to life — no design skills required.

      Great powers come with great privacy. Apple Intelligence is designed with privacy at its core. On-device processing means it understands personal context without collecting personal data — so your company’s information stays private and secure. For more complex tasks, Private Cloud Compute uses larger models running on Apple silicon servers with industry-leading protections. Your data is never stored or accessible to anyone — not even Apple.

    • Mac + iPhone. Better together.

      Mac + iPhone

      Mac + iPhone. Better together. Using Mac with other Apple devices lets you and your workforce do incredible things. Copy text, images, photos, and videos on your iPhone and paste to your Mac — or vice versa. Start an email on iPhone during the commute and finish it on Mac when you get to the office. Use AirDrop to transfer files from your Mac to a coworker down the hall. Or unlock your Mac simply by wearing your Apple Watch.

    The it computer for IT.

    • Easy to manage.
      Zero touch —
      and go.

      Device Management

      Easy to manage. Zero touch — and go. You can provision a Mac in just minutes with zero-touch deployment. From configuring devices to deploying apps to monitoring threats, IT can do it all via mobile device management without interrupting your team’s work or compromising their privacy.1

    • Priceless security. 
      Free of charge.

      Security

      Priceless security. Free of charge. With enterprise-grade security at every level, Mac protects you from malware and viruses, keeps data encrypted, and puts passwords at the tip of your finger with Touch ID. Free, automatic security updates allow everyone to stay focused on deliverables, without unexpected downtime.

      go the distance.

      Durability + Reliability

      Designed to go the distance. Made of seriously strong, 100 percent recycled aluminum, Mac computers are built to last. With fewer tech problems like glitches and crashes, your team can avoid unnecessary interruptions and stay productive.8 And free, automatic operating system updates keep your system running smoothly. So every Mac remains fast and responsive — now and into the future.

    Employees love Mac. Employers will too.

    • Never pay for
      macOS updates.

      macOS

      Never pay for macOS updates. macOS is the operating system that powers every Mac. It’s simple to navigate and more familiar than you might think, with built-in productivity and accessibility features. And free, seamless updates keep things running smoothly so your team can tackle their to-dos — not IT issues.

    • Apps that help you
      get down to business.

      Apps

      Apps that help you get down to business. Mac comes loaded with powerful apps that help your teams fulfill virtually any task. And it runs the familiar apps they already use — like Microsoft 365 Copilot, Google Workspace, SAP Business Suite, The OmniPlan Productivity Suite, and more. And just wait till they see how fast, smooth, and snappy Slack, Zoom, Box, and Google Docs can be. Still need more? There are nearly two million apps for business available on the App Store.9

      Learn more about apps for work

    • Give your
      people

      what they want.

      Employees Prefer Mac

      Give your people what they want. Companies that support Mac have a much higher degree of employee satisfaction. In fact, in a recent study, 60 percent of employees say they would like to be given the choice to work on Mac.10
